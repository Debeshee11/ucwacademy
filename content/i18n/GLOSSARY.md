# Translation glossary (formal style)

Style chosen by the owner: **formal Hindi and Gujarati**. Use the formal word for every term. Keep these as they are, because everyone already uses them: brand and product names (UpperCrust, Google, WhatsApp), acronyms (SIP, NAV, SEBI, AMFI, NISM, KYC, EMI, ETF, AUM, TDS, PAN, REIT, PMS, AIF, SIF), and everyday technology words (साइन इन, ईमेल, मोबाइल, पासवर्ड, ऐप, कैलकुलेटर / સાઇન ઇન, ઇમેઇલ, મોબાઇલ, પાસવર્ડ, એપ, કેલ્ક્યુલેટર).
On first use in a lesson, write the formal term then the acronym in brackets: व्यवस्थित निवेश योजना (SIP).
Numbers stay in Western digits (1, 2, 3) and ₹ stays as ₹. Placeholders such as {0} and {1} never change.
Address the learner as आप / તમે. Sentences short; no word-for-word English order.
