Key ideas you will take away

  • High return and guaranteed safety never come together.
  • Hurry is a warning sign. Real opportunities wait.
  • OTP, PIN, CVV, password: never shared, with anyone, ever.
  • UPI PIN is only for sending money, never for receiving.
  • “Digital arrest” does not exist. No agency demands money on a call.
  • Profits shown in an app are not money until they reach your bank.

Try a question

Q1. An agent offers Lata a plan that “doubles your money in 12 months, fully guaranteed”. Which response shows the best judgement?

  1. Invest a small amount first to test whether the payouts arrive
  2. Decline: no honest investment can guarantee doubling in a year
  3. Invest, but only if a relative has already been paid by the plan
  4. Ask the agent for a slightly lower return so that it becomes safer
Show the answer

B. Doubling in a year means a 100% return. A guarantee of that is the clearest sign of fraud. No amount is safe to “test” it with.

Q2. Ravi is selling a sofa online. The buyer says, “I have sent ₹8,000. Just approve the request and enter your UPI PIN to receive it.” What is actually happening?

  1. The buyer is paying through a newer and faster UPI feature for sellers
  2. The payment is on hold until Ravi verifies his identity
  3. The bank needs the PIN to confirm that Ravi owns the account
  4. Ravi is being asked to approve a payment from his own account
Show the answer

D. Receiving money on UPI never needs a PIN. A request that asks for one is a collect request: entering the PIN sends ₹8,000 from Ravi to the “buyer”.
