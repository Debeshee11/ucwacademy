Key ideas you will take away

  • Aggressive 65-80% equity.
  • Conservative 10-25% equity.
  • Cash buy, futures sell.
  • Hedged, so low volatility.
  • Net equity moves with valuation.
  • Smoother, not safe.

Try a question

Q1. An aggressive hybrid fund holds how much in equity?

  1. 10% to 25%
  2. 65% to 80%
  3. Over 90%
  4. Zero
Show the answer

B. The category range is 65% to 80% equity.

Q2. An arbitrage fund earns by:

  1. Picking undervalued stocks
  2. Capturing the gap between cash and futures prices
  3. Lending to companies
  4. Holding gold
Show the answer

B. It buys in cash and sells futures to lock in the spread.
