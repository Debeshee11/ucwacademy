Specialised Investment Funds (SIF)

SEBI’s newer category between mutual funds and PMS or AIFs: strategies, the ₹10 lakh threshold, long-short exposure and the risks.

Key ideas you will take away

  • Run by a mutual fund house.
  • Minimum ₹10 lakh.
  • Long-short uses both buying and selling.
  • Derivatives enable the short side.
  • SIF ₹10 lakh; PMS ₹50 lakh; AIF ₹1 crore.
  • SIF has a NAV like a fund.

Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “Specialised Investment Fund”?

  1. Suitability means matching a product to an investor’s goals, risk capacity and understanding, before recommending it.
  2. A SIF is a SEBI-regulated product offered by mutual fund houses with greater investment flexibility than regular funds, for investors who can take on more risk.
  3. A SIF requires a minimum investment of ₹10 lakh per investor, counted across all SIF strategies of that fund house.
  4. A SIF is positioned between a mutual fund and a PMS or AIF: more flexible than the first, more regulated and accessible than the others.
Show the answer

B. Specialised Investment Fund: A SIF is a SEBI-regulated product offered by mutual fund houses with greater investment flexibility than regular funds, for investors who can take on more risk.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Long-short strategy”?

  1. The tax treatment of a SIF depends on its asset mix and the holding period, so investors should check the latest rules before investing.
  2. Disclosure is the information a fund must provide, such as the scheme document, strategy, risks and portfolio, so investors can decide.
  3. A long-short strategy buys securities expected to rise and takes short positions in those expected to fall, aiming to gain in both directions.
  4. An equity long-short fund takes long positions in shares and uses derivatives to take short exposure, targeting returns across market conditions.
Show the answer

C. Long-short strategy: A long-short strategy buys securities expected to rise and takes short positions in those expected to fall, aiming to gain in both directions.
