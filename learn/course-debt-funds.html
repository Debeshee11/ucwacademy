Key ideas you will take away

  • Coupon fixed; price and yield move.
  • G-sec = sovereign; corporate = credit risk.
  • Rates up, prices down.
  • Longer maturity, bigger swing.
  • Price change ≈ −MD × Δyield.
  • Duration = rate risk.

Try a question

Q1. A bond has a face value of ₹100 and a 7% coupon. What coupon does it pay each year?

  1. ₹7
  2. ₹70
  3. ₹0.7
  4. ₹107
Show the answer

A. 7% of ₹100 = ₹7 a year.

Q2. Market interest rates rise. What generally happens to the price of an existing bond?

  1. It falls
  2. It rises
  3. It stays the same
  4. It doubles
Show the answer

A. New bonds pay more, so older lower-coupon bonds must sell at a lower price to match.
