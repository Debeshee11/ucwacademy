Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “Retirement corpus”?

  1. This is the percentage of your savings you draw each year, a rule of thumb and not a guarantee.
  2. This is the rise in prices over time, which reduces what each rupee can buy.
  3. This is the chance of outliving your savings.
  4. This is the total savings you need at retirement to support your expenses for the rest of your life.
Show the answer

D. Retirement corpus: A retirement corpus is the total savings you need at retirement to support your expenses for the rest of your life.

Q2. Which statement best describes “EPF”?

  1. This is the public provident fund, a government-backed long-term savings account with a rate set periodically.
  2. This is the employees’ provident fund, where employee and employer contribute a share of salary towards retirement.
  3. This is voluntary provident fund, where an employee adds extra contribution to the provident fund beyond the required share.
  4. This is the 15-year term after which the account matures, with options to extend.
Show the answer

B. EPF: EPF is the employees’ provident fund, where employee and employer contribute a share of salary towards retirement.
