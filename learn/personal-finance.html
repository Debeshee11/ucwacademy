Frequently asked questions

Where should I start with personal finance?

Start with a budget and an emergency fund, then cover insurance, clear costly debt, and only then begin investing through a SIP.

How much should an emergency fund be?

A common rule is three to six months of essential expenses, kept in a safe and easy-to-withdraw place such as a savings account or liquid fund.

Is this personal finance course free?

Yes. All lessons, quizzes and practice are free. Sign in once with your mobile number or Google.

Can I learn personal finance in Hindi or Gujarati?

Partly. Every screen, menu and button switches between English, Hindi and Gujarati, and about a third of the lesson quiz questions have Hindi and Gujarati versions. Lesson text and NISM mock tests are in English for now.
