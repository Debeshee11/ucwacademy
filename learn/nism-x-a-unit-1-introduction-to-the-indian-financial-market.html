Key terms, examples and exam traps

Financial system

The financial system consists of institutions, markets, instruments and regulators that move savings to productive uses.

Example. Banks, markets and insurers together channel household savings into investments.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The financial system consists of banks only.

Financial market

A financial market is where financial assets are created and traded, and it is divided into money market, capital market and foreign exchange market.

Example. Equity shares, short-term paper and currencies each trade in their own market.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Financial markets deal only in physical commodities.

RBI

The Reserve Bank of India is the central bank. It regulates banks, manages monetary policy and the money market, and issues currency.

Example. The authority that sets the repo rate also regulates banks.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The RBI regulates mutual funds.

IRDAI

The Insurance Regulatory and Development Authority of India regulates insurers and intermediaries and protects policyholders.

Example. A complaint about an insurance company's conduct is escalated to this body.

Exam trap. This statement is false: IRDAI regulates stock exchanges.

PFRDA

The Pension Fund Regulatory and Development Authority regulates the National Pension System and pension funds.

Example. The body that oversees NPS fund managers and subscribers.

Exam trap. This statement is false: PFRDA regulates mutual funds.

Repo rate

The repo rate is the rate at which the RBI lends short-term funds to banks against government securities, and it is the main policy rate.

Example. When the central bank raises the rate at which banks borrow, loan rates tend to rise.

Exam trap. This statement is false: A repo rate cut makes loans costlier.

Inflation and interest rates

When inflation rises, central banks tend to raise rates to cool demand, which affects bond prices and borrowing costs.

Example. Rising prices lead to higher policy rates and lower bond prices.

Exam trap. This statement is false: High inflation always leads to lower interest rates.

Primary dealer

Primary dealers are institutions that underwrite and trade government securities and support the central bank in managing borrowing.

Example. An institution that bids at government auctions and makes a market in G-secs.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Primary dealers sell only equity shares.

Stock exchanges and depositories

Exchanges provide the trading platform; depositories hold securities in electronic form; clearing corporations settle trades.

Example. A trade flows through an exchange, a clearing corporation and a depository.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Depositories execute trades on exchanges.

Foreign portfolio investor

A foreign portfolio investor is an overseas entity that invests in Indian securities, registered under SEBI's regulations.

Example. An overseas fund buys listed Indian shares and bonds.

Exam trap. This statement is false: An FPI is an Indian retail investor.

Credit rating agency

A credit rating agency assesses the creditworthiness of issuers and their debt instruments and is regulated by SEBI.

Example. An independent agency rates a company's bonds as AA+.

Exam trap. This statement is false: A credit rating agency issues the bonds.

Money supply and liquidity

Liquidity in the system is influenced by the RBI's tools such as CRR, SLR and open market operations.

Example. The central bank buys securities to inject liquidity into the system.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Liquidity is controlled only by the stock exchanges.
