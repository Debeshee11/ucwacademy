Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “NRI under FEMA”?

  1. This is a tax status where mainly income from India, plus some foreign income linked to India, is taxed.
  2. This is decided each year by counting days spent in India, and it decides whether global income is taxed in India.
  3. This is a person of Indian origin or citizenship who lives outside India for work, business, study or another purpose of uncertain duration.
  4. This is a foreign citizen of Indian origin who holds a lifelong Overseas Citizen of India card, and is treated like an NRI for many banking and investment rules.
Show the answer

C. NRI under FEMA: NRI under FEMA is a person of Indian origin or citizenship who lives outside India for work, business, study or another purpose of uncertain duration.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Global income”?

  1. This is income earned anywhere in the world, and residents are taxed on it in India.
  2. This means interest on an NRE account is generally free of Indian tax while the holder remains a non-resident.
  3. This is tax deducted at source on payments to NRIs, often at higher rates than for residents.
  4. This is income earned or received in India, such as rent, capital gains on Indian assets and NRO interest, and it is taxable for non-residents.
Show the answer

A. Global income: Global income is income earned anywhere in the world, and residents are taxed on it in India.
