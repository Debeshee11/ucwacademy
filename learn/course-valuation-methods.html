Key ideas you will take away

Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “Intrinsic value”?

  1. This is the price at which a share trades on the exchange right now.
  2. This is the idea that a rupee today is worth more than a rupee received later.
  3. This is the gap between the estimated value and the price paid, which protects against errors.
  4. This is the estimated worth of a business based on the cash it can generate in future.
Show the answer

D. Intrinsic value: Intrinsic value is the estimated worth of a business based on the cash it can generate in future.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Free cash flow to the firm”?

  1. This is the cash a business generates after expenses and investment, available to all its capital providers.
  2. This is the weighted average cost of a company's debt and equity, often used as the discount rate.
  3. This is the estimated worth of all cash flows beyond the forecast period.
  4. This is the yearly return used to convert future cash into today's value, reflecting risk.
Show the answer

A. Free cash flow to the firm: Free cash flow to the firm is the cash a business generates after expenses and investment, available to all its capital providers.
