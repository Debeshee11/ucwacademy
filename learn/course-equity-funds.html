Key ideas you will take away

  • Top 100 / 101-250 / 251+.
  • Large 80%, mid and small 65% minimum.
  • Multi cap: 25/25/25 minimum.
  • Flexi: manager's choice.
  • 3-year lock-in.
  • Old regime only for 80C.

Try a question

Q1. Under AMFI's list, mid caps are companies ranked:

  1. 1 to 100
  2. 251 to 500
  3. 501 to 750
  4. 101 to 250 by market cap
Show the answer

D. Large = 1-100, mid = 101-250, small = 251 onward.

Q2. A multi cap fund must hold at least how much in each of large, mid and small caps?

  1. 10%
  2. 33%
  3. 25%
  4. 50%
Show the answer

C. SEBI requires a minimum 25% in each segment.
