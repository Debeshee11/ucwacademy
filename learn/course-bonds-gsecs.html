Key ideas you will take away

  • T-bills are short term.

Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “Bond”?

  1. This is the date on which the bond’s principal is repaid.
  2. This is the amount printed on a bond that is repaid at maturity.
  3. This is a loan an investor gives to a government or company, which promises interest and the return of the principal.
  4. This is the regular interest a bond pays, usually stated as a percentage of face value.
Show the answer

C. Bond: A bond is a loan an investor gives to a government or company, which promises interest and the return of the principal.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Yield to maturity”?

  1. This is the chance that interest received must be reinvested at lower rates.
  2. This measures how sensitive a bond’s price is to changes in interest rates, where a higher figure means more sensitivity.
  3. This is the chance that rising rates push bond prices down.
  4. This is the annual return you earn if you buy a bond at its current price and hold it to maturity.
Show the answer

D. Yield to maturity: Yield to maturity is the annual return you earn if you buy a bond at its current price and hold it to maturity.
