Try a question

Q1. In an SIP, a lower NAV means:

  1. You get fewer units
  2. The instalment is cancelled
  3. You get more units for the same amount
  4. Exit load rises
Show the answer

C. Fixed money buys more units when prices are low.

Q2. A step-up SIP:

  1. Reduces the instalment each year
  2. Raises the instalment on a schedule
  3. Stops after one year
  4. Is only for debt funds
Show the answer

B. The instalment increases by a fixed amount or percentage.
