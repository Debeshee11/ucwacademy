Key ideas you will take away

  • Farmland is restricted.

Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “NRI KYC”?

  1. This means the NRE or NRO account the money comes from, which decides whether proceeds can be sent abroad.
  2. This is a limit some fund houses place on investors living in certain countries because of local regulations.
  3. This is tax deducted when an NRI redeems fund units, at rates set for non-residents.
  4. This is the identity and address check, including passport, visa and overseas address, needed before investing.
Show the answer

D. NRI KYC: NRI KYC is the identity and address check, including passport, visa and overseas address, needed before investing.

Q2. Which statement best describes “PIS account”?

  1. This is investing through NRE funds so that sale proceeds can be sent abroad.
  2. This is an account with an RBI-authorised bank that lets an NRI buy and sell shares on Indian exchanges under the Portfolio Investment Scheme.
  3. This is investing through NRO funds, where sending proceeds abroad is subject to limits and paperwork.
  4. This is a cap on how much of a single company foreign investors, including NRIs, may own together.
Show the answer

B. PIS account: A PIS account is an account with an RBI-authorised bank that lets an NRI buy and sell shares on Indian exchanges under the Portfolio Investment Scheme.
