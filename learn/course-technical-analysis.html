Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “Candlestick”?

  1. This shows the open, high, low and close of a share for one time period.
  2. This is the general direction of price over time, which can be up, down or sideways.
  3. This is the number of shares traded in a period.
  4. This is the length of time each candle on a chart covers, such as a day or a week.
Show the answer

A. Candlestick: A candlestick shows the open, high, low and close of a share for one time period.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Support”?

  1. This is a price level where selling has tended to stop a rise.
  2. This is a price level where buying has tended to stop a fall.
  3. This is a move of price through a support or resistance level, ideally with strong volume.
  4. This is a chart pattern of three peaks with the middle one highest, often seen as a sign of a trend reversal.
Show the answer

B. Support: Support is a price level where buying has tended to stop a fall.
