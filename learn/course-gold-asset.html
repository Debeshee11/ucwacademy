Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “Store of value”?

  1. Carat measures gold purity. 24 carat is almost pure gold, while 22 carat is about 91.6% gold mixed with other metals for strength.
  2. A store of value keeps its purchasing power over long periods. Gold has long served this role across cultures and eras.
  3. Safe-haven demand is investors moving into gold during uncertainty, war or financial stress, which can lift its price.
  4. Digital gold is gold bought online from a platform that holds the metal for you. It is not regulated by SEBI, so the seller’s credibility matters.
Show the answer

B. Store of value: A store of value keeps its purchasing power over long periods. Gold has long served this role across cultures and eras.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Real interest rate”?

  1. India adds import duty and a GST on gold, so the local price differs from the international price converted to rupees.
  2. The real interest rate is the interest rate minus inflation. Gold pays no interest, so higher real rates make it less attractive.
  3. Gold monetisation means depositing idle gold with a bank to earn interest instead of keeping it in a locker.
  4. A gold ETF is a fund that holds gold and trades on the stock exchange like a share. Its price tracks the market price of gold.
Show the answer

B. Real interest rate: The real interest rate is the interest rate minus inflation. Gold pays no interest, so higher real rates make it less attractive.
