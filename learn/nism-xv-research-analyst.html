Unit 15 notes and practice questions

Try 4 sample questions

These are from Mock 1. Open the free mock to see the correct answer and the reason behind each one.

Q1. Which statement best describes “Timeliness and updates”?

  1. Receivable days measure the average time taken to collect payment from customers.
  2. Reports should be updated when material events occur and should state the date and data cut-off.
  3. A simple moving average is the average closing price over a set number of periods, smoothing price data to show the trend.
  4. SEBI regulates the securities market, RBI the money market and banks, and exchanges oversee their members.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Stock-to-use ratio”?

  1. The weighted average cost of capital blends the cost of equity and after-tax cost of debt in proportion to their weights, and is the discount rate for FCFF.
  2. Independent directors on the board and its committees provide oversight free from management and promoter influence.
  3. The record date is the date on which a shareholder must be on the company's register to be eligible for a corporate benefit.
  4. The stock-to-use ratio compares closing stocks with consumption; low levels signal tightness and price pressure.

Q3. Which term is described here? Frequent changes of auditors, qualified audit opinions and delays in results can signal accounting or governance problems.

  1. Auditor red flags
  2. Prohibited compensation
  3. Corporate governance
  4. Leading indicators

Q4. Which term is described here? Government policy, taxes and regulation can change industry profitability, such as price controls in pharma or spectrum charges in telecom.

  1. Rating and price target history
  2. Regulation and policy
  3. Bottom-up approach
  4. Receivable days
