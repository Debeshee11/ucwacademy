Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “Estate”?

  1. This is how assets pass under personal law when a person dies without a valid will.
  2. This is a court document that confirms who can claim certain assets such as bank balances and securities of the deceased.
  3. This is a person entitled by law or a will to inherit from the deceased.
  4. This is everything a person owns at death, such as property, savings, investments and belongings.
Show the answer

D. Estate: An estate is everything a person owns at death, such as property, savings, investments and belongings.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Will”?

  1. This are people who watch the maker sign a will and sign it themselves, and the law requires at least two.
  2. This is the person named in a will to carry out its instructions.
  3. This is a document that changes or adds to an existing will.
  4. This is a legal declaration of how a person wants their assets to pass after death.
Show the answer

D. Will: A will is a legal declaration of how a person wants their assets to pass after death.
