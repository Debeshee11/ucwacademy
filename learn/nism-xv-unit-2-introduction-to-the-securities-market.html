Key terms, examples and exam traps

Securities market regulators

SEBI regulates the securities market, RBI the money market and banks, and exchanges oversee their members.

Example. A research report on listed shares is subject to the capital-market regulator.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The RBI regulates research analysts.

Market segments

The securities market has equity, debt, derivatives and commodity segments, each with its own instruments and participants.

Example. An analyst may cover shares, bonds and futures contracts.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Securities markets have only an equity segment.

Listing and disclosure

Listed companies must make continuous disclosures under the SEBI listing regulations, which are the primary source of information for analysts.

Example. A company files its quarterly results and announces material events on the exchange.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Listed companies need not disclose results to the public.
