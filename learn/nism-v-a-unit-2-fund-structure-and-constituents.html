Fund structure and constituents: NISM Series V-A notes and practice questions

In India a mutual fund has a three-tier structure of sponsor, trust and asset management company, supported by service providers. This unit teaches who does what, and who checks whom.

Key terms, examples and exam traps

Sponsor

The sponsor is the person or entity that sets up the mutual fund and establishes the trust. It must meet SEBI's track-record and net-worth conditions.

Example. A bank group creates a new fund house and registers its trust with SEBI.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The sponsor manages the investments of each scheme day to day.

Trustees

Trustees hold the fund's property in trust for unit holders and supervise the AMC. At least two-thirds of the trustees must be independent of the sponsor.

Example. A board looks after investor interests and reviews the AMC's compliance reports.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Trustees decide which stocks the schemes buy.

Asset Management Company (AMC)

The AMC is appointed by the trustees to manage the schemes' investments and operations. At least half of its directors must be independent of the sponsor.

Example. A company runs the fund's portfolios and employs the fund managers.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The AMC holds the securities of the schemes.

Custodian

The custodian safekeeps the securities and settles the trades of the schemes, and is independent of the sponsor.

Example. An institution holds the scheme's shares in its safekeeping and handles delivery.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The custodian selects which securities the fund buys.

Registrar and Transfer Agent (RTA)

The RTA maintains unit holders' records, processes purchases and redemptions, and sends statements on behalf of the AMC.

Example. An agency updates investor folios and dispatches account statements.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The RTA calculates the portfolio's investment strategy.

Fund accountant and auditor

The fund accountant maintains the scheme's books and computes NAV, while auditors independently audit the accounts.

Example. A firm prepares the scheme's accounts and an independent firm audits them.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The AMC may appoint the sponsor's auditor to the scheme.

Sponsor's contribution

The sponsor must contribute at least 40% of the net worth of the AMC.

Example. A sponsor holds 40% of the AMC's net worth when the fund house is set up.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The sponsor need not contribute to the AMC's net worth.

Mutual fund as a trust

In India a mutual fund is constituted as a public trust under the Indian Trusts Act, and the trust is registered with SEBI.

Example. A fund house sets up its mutual fund as a registered public trust.

Exam trap. This statement is false: A mutual fund in India is incorporated as a company.
