Try a question

Q1. An investor has long-term equity fund gains of ₹5,00,000 in a year and no other gains. At 12.5% above a ₹1,25,000 exemption, what is the tax (before cess)?

  1. ₹46,875
  2. ₹62,500
  3. ₹78,125
  4. ₹1,25,000
Show the answer

A. (₹5,00,000 − ₹1,25,000) × 12.5% = ₹46,875.

Q2. What does a switch from one mutual fund scheme to another amount to for tax purposes?

  1. A redemption followed by a purchase
  2. No tax event
  3. A gift
  4. A bonus issue
Show the answer

A. Switches are treated as a sale of units and a new purchase.
