Try a question

Q1. Loss aversion means:

  1. Gains feel worse than losses
  2. People ignore losses
  3. Investors prefer risk
  4. Losses feel worse than equal gains feel good
Show the answer

D. The pain of loss is stronger than the pleasure of gain.

Q2. Recency bias leads people to:

  1. Ignore recent performance
  2. Expect recent performance to continue
  3. Prefer old funds
  4. Avoid all funds
Show the answer

B. They give too much weight to the latest results.
