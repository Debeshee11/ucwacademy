How to read this

  • Pass % is the share of total marks you need. Series V-A needs 50%, most others need 60%.
  • Negative marking takes marks off a wrong answer in some exams. Where it applies, leave a question blank if you are unsure.
  • Where there is no negative marking, attempt every question.

Aim for 15 marks above the pass mark in your mocks before you book. See a 4-week plan.

Frequently asked questions

What is the passing mark in NISM Series V-A?

50% of the total marks, with no negative marking.

Is there negative marking in NISM exams?

It depends on the exam. Series V-A and XII have none. Several others, such as VIII and XV, have 25% negative marking.

How many questions are in a NISM exam?

Most have 100 questions in 120 minutes. The longer exams, such as X-B and XXI-B, run 3 hours.

How many marks should I score in mocks?

Aim for about 15 marks above the pass mark before you book the real exam.
