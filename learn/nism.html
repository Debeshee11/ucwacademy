Frequently asked questions

Which NISM exam should I take?

It depends on the role. Mutual fund distributors look at Series V-A, those new to markets at Series XII, and advisers and analysts at the advice and research exams listed below. Use the table to compare each exam.

Are these NISM mock tests free?

Yes. UpperCrust Academy gives free mock tests and a free study guide PDF for all 13 exams listed here.

How do I prepare for a NISM exam?

Read the study guide once, study the heaviest units first, then take mock tests. Move on only when you clear the pass mark by 15 marks, and redo every wrong answer.

Is the study material available in Hindi or Gujarati?

The app, including its lessons and quizzes, can be switched between English, Hindi and Gujarati. The study guide PDFs are in English.

Is UpperCrust Academy part of NISM?

No. It is independent preparation material from UpperCrust Wealth. Check the official NISM website for the latest syllabus and rules.
