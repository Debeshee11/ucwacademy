Q1. Which term is described here? Nomination lets an account holder name a person who will receive the securities if the holder dies.

  1. Nomination
  2. Demat request form
  3. Market intermediaries
  4. Inter-depository transfer

Q2. Which statement best describes “Registered owner”?

  1. Government securities (G-secs) are debt instruments issued by the central or state governments, and they carry the lowest credit risk.
  2. In the issuer's books the depository appears as the registered owner of dematerialised securities but holds them for the beneficial owner.
  3. Securities may be delivered at the usual pay-in time or earlier, called early pay-in, and the DP processes instructions before the deadline.
  4. SEBI does not permit the transfer of listed securities in physical form, other than for transmission and transposition, so securities must be dematerialised before transfer.

Q3. Which statement best describes “Inspection and supervision”?

  1. An issuer is the company or body that issues securities and may offer them in demat form through the depository.
  2. Clients give securities as margin to brokers by creating a margin pledge in favour of the clearing member, and the clearing member may re-pledge them to the clearing corporation.
  3. Depositories inspect DPs, monitor compliance and can take action for violations, and SEBI oversees the system.
  4. A DP must keep investors' securities separate from its own and cannot use client securities for its own benefit.

Q4. Which statement best describes “Types of accounts”?

  1. Demat accounts may be opened by individuals, joint holders, HUFs, companies, trusts, NRIs and minors through a guardian.
  2. Debt securities such as debentures and bonds are instruments by which an issuer borrows and pays interest, and can be held in demat form.
  3. An off-market transfer is a transfer of securities directly between two BOs without a trade on an exchange, such as a gift or family transfer.
  4. The registrar verifies the request and certificates, cancels them, confirms to the depository, and the depository credits the account.
