NISM exam guide

NISM V-A study plan: 4 weeks, day by day

Fifteen days for the units in proportion to their weight, then mocks, revision and rest.

The plan at a glance

  • Week 1–3: read the units, about one hour a day. The heaviest units get the most days.
  • Week 4: take the 5 mocks, redo every wrong answer, rest the day before.

Most candidates need two to four weeks for an exam like V-A. If you work full time, use the full four weeks.

Each unit page has notes, key terms, exam traps and practice questions.

Days 16–28: mocks and revision

  1. Day 16–17: take Mock 1. Note your weak units.
  2. Day 18–20: reread the weak units.
  3. Day 21–25: take Mocks 2 to 5, one a day.
  4. Day 26–27: redo every wrong answer. Each one names its unit.
  5. Day 28: rest. Do not study new material.

Book the real exam when you clear 65 or more in your mocks.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to prepare for NISM V-A?

Most candidates need two to four weeks of about an hour a day. This plan uses four weeks.

Which NISM V-A units matter most?

The units with the highest weight, listed in the plan table. Spend the most days on them.

How many mocks should I take?

Take all 5 full mocks and redo every wrong answer. Book the exam once you score about 65 or more.

Is the official NISM workbook free?

Yes. NISM gives a free soft copy after you enrol and lets you download it from its website.
