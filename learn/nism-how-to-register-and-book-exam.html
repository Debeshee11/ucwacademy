How to register and book a NISM exam

Six steps from profile to admit card, with what the exam costs and what to carry on exam day.

NISM exam registration: step by step

  1. Create your profile on the NISM certification portal at cert.nism.ac.in. Registration is online and the window is open all year.
  2. Log in and choose Enrolment for Certification. Pick the exam you need, for example NISM Series V-A.
  3. Check seat availability, then choose a test centre, a date and a time slot.
  4. Pay the exam fee online. Fees differ by exam, so read the amount on the payment page.
  5. Download your confirmation and admit card and keep a copy of the payment receipt.
  6. On exam day, carry the admit card and a valid photo ID such as Aadhaar, PAN, passport or driving licence.

NISM runs its exams at test centres in more than 150 cities, so book early to get the slot and centre you want.

What does a NISM exam cost?

The fee depends on the exam. NISM lists Series XXI-A at ₹1,500 including GST, and its certification pamphlet lists Series I at ₹1,500 and Series XIX-A at ₹1,770. Investment adviser exams cost more. Always read the fee on the portal on the day you book.

Study material is free

NISM sends a soft copy of the official workbook after you enrol, and you can also download it free from the NISM website. Our unit-wise study guides and free mocks work alongside it.

Before you book

  • Finish one full read and at least two mocks first. See the 4-week plan.
  • Not sure which exam to pick? Read which NISM exam to take.
  • Enter your name exactly as it appears on your ID.

Frequently asked questions

Is NISM registration open all year?

Yes. NISM exam registration and the testing window stay open throughout the year, so you can book when you are ready.

Where do I register for a NISM exam?

On the NISM certification portal, cert.nism.ac.in. After registration you choose a test centre, date and time slot and pay online.

Is the NISM study material free?

Yes. NISM gives a soft copy of the workbook after enrolment and also lets you download it free from its website.

How early should I book a NISM exam?

Book as early as you can. Popular centres and dates fill up, and you need time to finish your mocks first.
