Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “Research analyst”?

  1. This is the certification exam for research analysts, covering markets, analysis methods and the regulations.
  2. This is a written analysis of a company or security that gives a view and the reasons behind it.
  3. This is the licence a research analyst must hold from the securities regulator before offering research.
  4. This is a person who prepares or publishes research on securities, such as reports and recommendations.
Show the answer

D. Research analyst: A research analyst is a person who prepares or publishes research on securities, such as reports and recommendations.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Investment thesis”?

  1. This is an analyst's simple call on a security, such as buy, hold or sell.
  2. This is the central reason an analyst believes a share will do well or badly.
  3. This are the main things that could make the analyst's view wrong.
  4. This are the stated inputs behind a forecast, such as growth, margins and the discount rate.
Show the answer

B. Investment thesis: An investment thesis is the central reason an analyst believes a share will do well or badly.
