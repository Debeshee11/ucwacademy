Q1. Which term is described here? The CML shows the risk-return trade-off of efficient portfolios that combine the risk-free asset with the market portfolio.

  1. Bond immunisation
  2. Top-down analysis
  3. Capital market line
  4. Portfolio manager defined

Q2. Which term is described here? A clearing corporation guarantees settlement of trades and manages counterparty risk, with delivery of securities and funds through depositories and banks.

  1. Active strategy
  2. Multi-factor models
  3. Corporate governance
  4. Clearing and settlement

Q3. Which statement best describes “Front running”?

  1. SEBI requires portfolio manager performance to be reported on a uniform basis, with returns computed using TWRR through a central platform.
  2. Front running is trading ahead of a client's large order for the manager's own benefit and is a prohibited practice.
  3. Anomalies, such as size and value effects, are patterns that appear to contradict market efficiency.
  4. A portfolio manager may charge a fee that is fixed, return-based or a mix of both, but cannot charge an upfront fee directly or indirectly from clients.

Q4. Which statement best describes “Benchmark selection”?

  1. Smart beta uses rules-based weights based on factors such as value or low volatility instead of market capitalisation.
  2. A good benchmark is appropriate, investable, measurable and specified in advance, and reflects the manager's style.
  3. The disclosure document gives clients the manager's investment approach, fees, risks, performance and other facts, and is filed with SEBI.
  4. The yield curve plots yields against maturity, and an upward-sloping curve means longer maturities have higher yields.
