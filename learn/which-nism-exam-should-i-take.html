Not sure where to start?

Students and career changers usually begin with NISM Series XII, a broad market foundation. Anyone planning to sell mutual funds starts with Series V-A. Check your employer or the rule for your role before you pay for an exam.

Frequently asked questions

Which NISM exam is required to sell mutual funds?

NISM Series V-A, Mutual Fund Distributors.

Which NISM exam is required for a research analyst?

NISM Series XV, Research Analyst.

Which NISM exam is required for an investment adviser?

Investment advisers take NISM Series X-A (Level 1) and X-B (Level 2).

Which NISM exam is best for beginners?

NISM Series XII, Securities Markets Foundation, is a broad first exam. If you plan to distribute mutual funds, start with V-A.
