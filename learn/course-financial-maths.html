Key ideas you will take away

  • FV = PV × (1+r)^n.
  • Compare amounts on the same date.
  • Rule of 72 = 72 ÷ rate.
  • Compounding favours time.
  • CAGR = (End/Start)^(1/n) − 1.
  • Absolute return hides time.

Try a question

Q1. ₹1,00,000 grows at 10% a year for 2 years. What is the future value?

  1. ₹1,21,000
  2. ₹1,20,000
  3. ₹1,10,000
  4. ₹1,31,000
Show the answer

A. 1,00,000 × 1.1 × 1.1 = ₹1,21,000. Interest earns interest, so it is more than the ₹1,20,000 simple-interest answer.

Q2. Using the Rule of 72, about how long does money take to double at 9% a year?

  1. 8 years
  2. 4 years
  3. 12 years
  4. 18 years
Show the answer

A. 72 ÷ 9 = 8 years.
