Key ideas you will take away

  • A REIT is a listed trust that owns rent-earning property.
  • You own units, not a building.
  • 80% completed and rent-generating.
  • 90% of NDCF paid out, at least every six months.
  • Yield = annual distribution ÷ price.
  • Long WALE, high occupancy = steadier income.

Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes a REIT?

  1. A listed trust that owns income-producing real estate and passes most of its income to unitholders
  2. A fixed deposit with a real estate company
  3. A loan given to builders at a fixed rate
  4. A government scheme that guarantees rent
Show the answer

A. A REIT is a trust that owns rent-generating property and distributes income. It is a market-linked unit, not a guaranteed product.

Q2. Under SEBI rules, what minimum share of a REIT's assets must be in completed, rent-generating properties?

  1. 80%
  2. 50%
  3. 20%
  4. 100%
Show the answer

A. At least 80% must be in completed, income-producing assets. The balance can include under-construction projects within limits.
