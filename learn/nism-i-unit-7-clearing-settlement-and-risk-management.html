Key terms, examples and exam traps

Clearing corporation

The clearing corporation acts as the counterparty to every trade, guaranteeing its performance.

Example. The buyer and seller each deal with the clearing corporation.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Buyers and sellers deal directly with each other.

Initial margin

Initial margin is collected up front to cover the potential loss on a position over a short period.

Example. A client deposits margin before taking a position.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Initial margin is returned immediately.

SPAN margin

SPAN margin is computed by a risk-based method that estimates the worst loss across scenarios for the portfolio.

Example. The system calculates margin for combined positions.

Exam trap. This statement is false: SPAN margin is a fixed percentage of premium.

Extreme loss margin

An additional margin is collected to cover losses beyond the normal scenario.

Example. A buffer margin is added for extreme moves.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Extreme loss margin replaces initial margin.

Mark-to-market settlement

Daily mark-to-market profits and losses are settled in cash through the clearing banks.

Example. A losing member pays the loss the next morning.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Gains and losses are carried until expiry.

Daily settlement price

The daily settlement price is based on the weighted average price of the last half hour of trading.

Example. Positions are marked at the daily settlement price.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The settlement price is the opening price.

Final settlement

At expiry open positions are settled in cash at the final settlement price and the contract ceases.

Example. A position is closed automatically at expiry.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Open positions roll over automatically.

Margin call

If margin falls below the requirement, the member must top it up promptly or face position closure.

Example. A client receives a call to add funds.

Exam trap. This statement is false: A margin call is a request to withdraw funds.

Option premium settlement

Option premiums are paid by the buyer and received by the seller on the next day after the trade.

Example. A buyer pays the premium after the trade.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Premiums are paid at expiry.

Settlement guarantee fund

A fund maintained by the clearing corporation protects the market in the event of member default.

Example. The fund helps to complete settlement in a default.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The fund pays clients' trading profits.

Position limits and risk

Position limits for members and clients help control concentration and systemic risk.

Example. A member cannot hold positions above its cap.

Exam trap. This statement is false: Limits only apply to the exchange.

Default management

In a default, the clearing corporation closes positions, uses the defaulter's margin and then other resources as per a waterfall.

Example. The defaulter's margin is used first.

Exam trap. This statement is false: The clearing corporation ignores defaults.
