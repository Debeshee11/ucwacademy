Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “Total addressable market”?

  1. This is the share of possible customers who already use a product.
  2. This is a factor that makes customers buy more, such as rising incomes or new rules.
  3. This is the full amount of sales a product could earn if it reached every possible buyer.
  4. This is the portion of an industry's sales that one company holds.
Show the answer

C. Total addressable market: Total addressable market is the full amount of sales a product could earn if it reached every possible buyer.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Porter's five forces”?

  1. This is the ability to raise prices without losing many customers.
  2. This is a framework that judges industry competition by looking at rivals, new entrants, substitutes, buyers and suppliers.
  3. This are costs or rules that make it hard for new firms to join an industry.
  4. This is a lasting advantage that protects a company's profits from rivals.
Show the answer

B. Porter's five forces: Porter's five forces is a framework that judges industry competition by looking at rivals, new entrants, substitutes, buyers and suppliers.
