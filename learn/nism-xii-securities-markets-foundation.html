NISM Series XII syllabus, unit by unit

The exam covers 7 units. The percentage is the share of questions each unit usually carries, so spend your time in that proportion.

Unit 1 Understanding securities markets and performance5%

Securities markets move savings of households into the investments of companies and governments. This chapter explains why markets exist, who takes part, and who keeps them honest.

  • Primary versus secondary market.
  • Money market versus capital market.
  • Roles of SEBI, RBI and other regulators.
Unit 2 Securities: types, features and concepts20%

Equity, preference shares, bonds, government securities and money market instruments all have different rights and risks. This is the biggest topic and most questions are definition or comparison based.

  • Equity versus preference shares.
  • Bonds, debentures, G-secs, T-bills, CP and CD.
  • Corporate actions: dividend, bonus, split, rights, buyback.
  • Credit ratings and key valuation ratios.
Unit 3 Primary markets15%

How companies raise money from the public: IPOs, FPOs, rights, QIPs and private placements. The mechanics of book building, categories of investors, and listing are tested closely.

  • IPO process and documents.
  • Book building, price band and cut-off price.
  • Investor categories and reservation.
  • Other routes: rights, QIP, preferential, OFS.
Unit 4 Secondary markets20%

Where shares trade every day. This chapter covers exchanges, brokers, orders, indices, settlement, the depository system and the safeguards that protect the market.

  • Stock exchanges, trading hours and settlement.
  • Order types and price bands.
  • Depositories and demat.
  • Indices and circuit breakers.
Unit 5 Mutual funds20%

Mutual funds pool money from many investors. This chapter covers structure, NAV, scheme types, costs, SIP and what to read before investing.

  • Structure of a mutual fund and its NAV.
  • Scheme types: equity, debt, hybrid, index, ETF.
  • SIP, expense ratio, exit load and riskometer.
  • How to choose a fund.
Unit 6 Derivatives markets10%

Futures and options in simple terms: what they are, why people use them and the key vocabulary.

  • Futures versus options.
  • Hedging, speculation, arbitrage.
  • Lot size, margin and expiry.
Unit 7 Financial planning and securities markets10%

Markets are a means to meet goals. This chapter links securities to planning: goals, risk profile, asset allocation, tax, insurance and avoiding frauds.

  • Financial planning steps and life stages.
  • Risk profile and asset allocation.
  • Time value of money and inflation.
  • Investor rights, grievance routes and frauds.
