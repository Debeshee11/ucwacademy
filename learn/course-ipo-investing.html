Try a question

Q1. Which statement best describes “IPO”?

  1. This is the prospectus that describes the company, its business, risks and use of funds.
  2. This is where new shares are issued and sold by the company, as in an IPO.
  3. This is when a company sells its shares to the public for the first time and gets listed on an exchange.
  4. This is the exchange where existing shares are bought and sold among investors after listing.
Show the answer

C. IPO: An IPO, or initial public offering, is when a company sells its shares to the public for the first time and gets listed on an exchange.

Q2. Which statement best describes “Price band”?

  1. This is the range in which investors can bid for shares in the IPO.
  2. This is an option where a retail investor agrees to buy at whatever final price is set within the band.
  3. This is a way to apply where the amount is blocked in your bank account and debited only if shares are allotted.
  4. This is the process of collecting bids within the price band to discover the final issue price.
Show the answer

A. Price band: A price band is the range in which investors can bid for shares in the IPO.
