Key ideas you will take away

  • Track one full month before you change anything.
  • Fixed, variable, saved: every rupee goes in one pile.
  • Start with 50-30-20, then adjust it to your life.
  • Pay yourself first: savings leave on salary day, not month-end.
  • Essentials × 3 to 6 months. Go to 6 to 12 if income is irregular or you are the only earner.
  • Safe and reachable in a day: savings account, sweep-in deposit, liquid fund.

Try a question

Q1. Arjun takes home ₹80,000 a month. After all spending, ₹12,000 goes into savings and investments. What is his savings rate?

  1. 12%
  2. 15%
  3. 18%
  4. 20%
Show the answer

B. Savings rate is the amount saved divided by take-home pay: ₹12,000 ÷ ₹80,000 = 0.15, or 15%.

Q2. Kavita takes home ₹70,000 a month and wants to follow the 50-30-20 rule. How much should go to savings and debt repayment?

  1. ₹7,000
  2. ₹14,000
  3. ₹21,000
  4. ₹35,000
Show the answer

B. The 20 in 50-30-20 is the savings share. 20% of ₹70,000 is ₹14,000.
