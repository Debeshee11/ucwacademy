Key ideas you will take away

  • Fund = trust; AMC manages.
  • Custodian holds the securities.
  • Compare within category.
  • Riskometer shows risk, not return.
  • TER is inside the NAV.
  • Direct = no commission.

Try a question

Q1. Who holds the securities a mutual fund buys?

  1. The AMC's treasury team
  2. The distributor
  3. The custodian
  4. The registrar
Show the answer

C. A separate custodian holds the securities. That separation protects unit holders if the AMC has trouble.

Q2. Why does SEBI define fund categories?

  1. To guarantee returns
  2. To fix fund charges
  3. To stop funds changing manager
  4. So funds with the same label follow similar rules
Show the answer

D. Categories make funds of one label comparable and stop style drift. They do not promise returns.
